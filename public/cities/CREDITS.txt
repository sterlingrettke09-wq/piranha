── WHY THESE ARE STAGED AND NOT SHIPPED ───────────────────────────────────────

Three things turned up in the fetching that were not known when it was asked
for, and each one is a reason for a person rather than a script to decide:

  1. Two BCG filenames carry stock-agency asset ids, so those images are
     licensed in rather than owned — the same pattern McKinsey's whole library
     shows. Absence of an agency marker in the other filenames is NOT evidence
     of ownership; it is only absence of evidence.
  2. Several are not city views. Seattle is a pedestrian bridge; Chicago is an
     office banner. They would be a downgrade on the photos already shipping.
  3. Section 1 is a different KIND of provenance: a CC licence is a public grant
     attached to the work, checkable by anyone, forever. These rest on a private
     email. Both can be fine; only one can be audited by someone who was not on
     the thread — which is the test everything else in this repo is held to.

To ship any of them: move the file to public/cities/<slug>.jpg, move its line
into a 'in use' block here, and move the Section 1 line it replaces into a
superseded block rather than deleting it.
